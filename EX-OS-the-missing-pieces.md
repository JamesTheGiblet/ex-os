## 6. The Ex-OS Philosophy (Expanded)

### 6.1 Sovereignty is Physical

The system runs entirely on your hardware:

- **UEFI USB** — boots before the OS loads
- **ESP32-C3** — sensory nerves, sub-50kb footprint
- **Ryzen 3300U** — master node, 16GB RAM
- **VPS (Hetzner)** — cloud brain, always on
- **S24 Ultra (Termux)** — mobile edge, development environment

**No cloud. No API calls. No data leaving your possession.**

### 6.2 Trust is Earned, Not Granted

New entities start at λ = 1.00 (neutral).

Trust is earned through:

- Independent corroboration
- Rule firing conclusions
- Auditor endorsement

Trust is lost through:

- Time passing without re-verification
- Contradicting capsules
- Auditor challenge events

### 6.3 Accountability is Structural

Every action is:

1. **Declared** (SCP)
2. **Classified** (DataCube)
3. **Trust-scored** (Leighton Weight)
4. **Audited** (ChronoSCRIBE)
5. **Enforced** (HAL)

**Nothing happens without a seal. Nothing is sealed without verification.**

### 6.4 Learning is Automatic

Ex-OS improves itself without human intervention:

- **Leighton Weight** continuously recalibrates trust (λ)
- **Plasticity** continuously reroutes neural pathways
- **BuddAI** continuously consolidates memories
- **Replicant** continuously adapts to the environment

**The system learns from every observation.**

### 6.5 Healing is Automatic

When a component fails:

1. **ChronoSCRIBE** records the error
2. **Leighton Weight** quarantines the offender
3. **HAL** refuses to execute its actions
4. **Plasticity** routes around the damage
5. **Replicant** spawns alternative pathways

**The system heals itself by routing around brokenness.**
