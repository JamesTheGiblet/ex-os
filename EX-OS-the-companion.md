## 1. What This Is

Ex-OS is not just a system. It is a **companion**.

A companion that:

- **Remembers** what you forget (BuddAI, Mimir)
- **Trusts** what you verify (Leighton Weight)
- **Records** what you do (ChronoSCRIBE)
- **Enforces** what you decide (Keystone Gate, HAL)
- **Explores** what you wonder (Replicant)
- **Verifies** what you know (Anchor)
- **Packages** what you create (Axiom)
- **Runs** what you build (UBVM, UBVM-OS)
